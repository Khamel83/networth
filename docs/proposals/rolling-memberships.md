Status: parked, November 2, 2026. Nothing is decided or built. Ashley raised the idea for 2027; the owner will come back to it.

## The idea

Each member gets 12 months from when they pay, instead of everyone renewing in January. Members get reminders before their anniversary. A January recruitment push and the two Grand Slam watch parties stay.

## What is settled

- 2027 is the fresh start. No backdating to join dates, and nothing gets cleared.
- Social Butterflies expire when their paid period ends, like Players.
- January stays as it is until the design is agreed.
