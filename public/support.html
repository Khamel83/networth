Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join the league? +

Head to our Join page and fill out the form. Choose between Player ($35) or Social Butterfly ($45) membership. Once you submit and send payment via Venmo to @NCOFFEN, you're in!

How do monthly matches work? +

At the start of each month, Player members are paired with another player. You'll receive an email connecting you both. Just reply all to coordinate when and where to play.

What if I can't play this month? +

No problem! Log into your dashboard and click "Sit Out This Month". You won't be assigned matches until you toggle back in. Your ranking stays the same while you're away.

How do I report a match score? +

After your match, log into your dashboard and click "Report Score" on your current match. Enter both set scores (e.g., 6-4, 3-6). Either player can report.

What if we don't finish by month end? +

That's okay! Matches can be completed later. We'll send a friendly reminder, but there's no penalty. Just report the score whenever you finish playing.

How does ranking work? +

Rankings are based on total games won, not match wins. Every game counts! A close 7-6, 6-7 match gives both players nearly the same credit. This rewards competitive play.

How do I log in? +

Just enter your email on the login page and we'll send you a magic link. No password needed! Click the link in your email (from noreply@mail.app.supabase.io) and you're in.

What's the difference between Player and Social Butterfly? +

Player ($35/year): Monthly match assignments, score tracking, rankings, plus events and community.

Social Butterfly ($45/year): Events, clinics, watch parties, and community access without monthly match assignments.
